About Me

Life is like a race that I run in sprints. Whether it's a half marathon or a lap around the track, I'm always curious about what's behind the next turn and willing to let the road take me somewhere unexpected.

My path to engineering hasn't exactly been a straight line. I earned my first bachelor's degree in Political Science in 2022 and spent six years working in the public sector, building skills in communication, community building, and project management. But my curiosity for building, creating, and solving problems in a more hands-on way never left.

That curiosity brought me back to MSU Denver, where I am now studying engineering and computer science and exploring my interests in coding, CAD, electronics, aerospace, cars, and motorsports. When I'm not building or learning something new, you can usually find me running, exploring, looking at the stars, or spending time with my favorite pit crew. Welcome to life beyond the grid.

My Pit Crew

My pit crew consists of friends, family, and mentors who support me in my journey. They help me stay focused, motivated, and ready to tackle the next challenge.
